Privacy Policy for Brawl Toons

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Brawl Toons (the “Game”), developed by VibeDeV and published by VIBECORE DIGITAL (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play on iPhone and iPad. Brawl Toons is an offline-first, single-player action game — you do not create an account or sign in to play, and we do not operate our own servers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information needed to run the Game and its optional online features:

  • Local game data — your progress, coins, unlocked classes, high scores, selected character, and settings, stored only in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • In-app purchase status — whether you own products such as “Remove Ads” or the coin pack. Payments are processed securely by the Apple App Store; we never receive your card or payment details.
  • Advertising & diagnostic data — Google AdMob may collect your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) and technical device information to serve and measure ads. On iOS, any use of the IDFA for cross-app tracking is subject to your permission (see “Advertising” below).
  • Game Center identifier — if you use the optional Apple Game Center, your Game Center player ID is used to submit and display the Death Run leaderboard score. This is optional; the Game is fully playable without it.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Camera, microphone, contacts, or personal files
  • Social media accounts

3. How We Use Information

  • Save your progress, coins, and preferences on your device
  • Provide and improve the Game
  • Process in-app purchases securely through the Apple App Store
  • Serve and measure advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Submit and show your optional Apple Game Center leaderboard score

4. Data Storage

Your gameplay data is stored locally on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not build personal profiles of you. The Game plays entirely on-device; the network is used only for the optional third-party services below.

5. Third-Party Services

Brawl Toons uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own policy:

  • Apple Game Center — the optional Death Run leaderboard
  • Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) — in-app purchases
  • Google AdMob — advertising

See the Apple Privacy Policy, the Google Privacy Policy, and Google’s advertising policy for how each provider handles data.

6. Advertising

  • We display ads through Google AdMob.
  • AdMob may use an advertising identifier (IDFA) to serve and measure ads.
  • On iOS, apps must ask for your permission before tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency). If you do not grant permission, ads are served without the IDFA and are non-personalized.
  • In the EEA and UK, we also ask for your consent (via Google’s consent form) before serving personalized ads. You can review or withdraw it any time from the Manage Ad Consent button on the in-app Privacy page.
  • You can manage tracking and personalized ads in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (and Apple Advertising), or remove banner and interstitial ads entirely with the in-app “Remove Ads” purchase.

7. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Brawl Toons is family-friendly but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and no account or personal details are required to play. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

8. Data Security

In-app purchases are processed securely through the Apple App Store. We apply reasonable measures to protect the data stored on your device.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Delete your data: uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored game data.
  • Game Center: manage or sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings → Game Center.
  • Ad personalization & tracking: manage in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Questions: contact us about any privacy concern.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes are reflected in the “Last Updated” date above.
