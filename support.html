Frequently Asked Questions

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new device?

Remove Ads is a non-consumable purchase, so it is tied to your Apple Account and can be restored at no extra cost. Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. The 1000 Coins pack is consumable and is spent once used, so it is not restored.

I reinstalled the game and my coins and classes are gone.

Your progress, coins, unlocked fighter classes, high scores and settings are stored on your device only. We do not run servers and there is no account, which means there is no cloud copy to restore. Deleting the app deletes that data. Purchases are the exception: restore Remove Ads as described above.

The leaderboard is empty, or my score is not showing.

Check that your device is signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app. The global Death Run leaderboard also stays sparse until the app has been publicly released and other players have posted scores, so a new or recently released game can look empty for a while even when everything is working. Your own score should appear once Game Center is signed in.

How do I change my ad or tracking choices?

  • Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. This controls whether the advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Personalised ads consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the Manage Ad Consent button on the in-app Privacy page. That option only appears where consent is required.
  • Remove ads entirely: the Remove Ads purchase in the Shop clears the banner and the between-round ads.

Do I need an account to play?

No. There is no sign up, no login and no personal details required. The game is fully playable offline. Game Center is optional and only affects the leaderboard.
